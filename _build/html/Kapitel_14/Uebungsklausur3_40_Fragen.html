D) 7

Frage 39

f = open('data.txt', ???)

A) 'r'

B) 'w'

C) 'a'

D) 'x'

Frage 40

Was ist die Ausgabe?

import tempfile, os

with tempfile.NamedTemporaryFile(mode='w', suffix='.txt', delete=False) as f:
    f.write('hello')
    name = f.name

with open(name, 'r') as f:
    content = f.read()

os.unlink(name)
print(content)

A) hello

B) None

C) ''

D) FileNotFoundError
